Listening Notes Playlist

Read each note as the song plays to follow key ideas and moments.

  1. Intro Drift — Aurora Lane

    Notice the soft synth layers building from left to right in your headphones.

  2. Midnight Signals — The Static

    Focus on the bass line in the chorus; it carries the tension of the whole track.

  3. City Lights, Slow Motion — Nocturne Club

    The second verse adds subtle backing vocals—listen for the call-and-response pattern.

  4. Homeward — Ember Coast

    The final minute strips back to piano and voice; this is the emotional payoff.
